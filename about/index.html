About

John O. Ezekiel

Agricultural & Environmental Engineer developing into agricultural and environmental data analytics, with a focus on food security, agricultural production, and environmental systems.

Engineering Foundation

John holds a B.Eng. in Agricultural & Environmental Engineering from the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), completing the academic programme from 2019 to 2024 and graduating in 2025 following a COVID-19-related academic disruption. Relevant academic areas include Environmental Engineering, Soil & Water Conservation, Farm Power & Machinery, Waste Management Systems, and Irrigation & Drainage Systems.

Practical Exposure

In 2024, John completed an Engineering Internship at the National Centre for Agricultural Mechanization (NCAM) in Ilorin, Kwara State, with primary exposure to Farm Power & Machinery. This included participation in three-wheel tractor assembly and setup, coupling machinery, machinery setup and testing, fabrication of farm implements including planter and potato-planter work, combine-harvester-related work, agricultural machinery workshop operations, equipment testing, and technical documentation — alongside exposure to solar-powered water systems, biomass/briquette production, and biogas/cooking-gas production.

From 2023 to 2024, John also worked as a Mechanical Intern at an auto diagnostic workshop in Oyo State, gaining exposure to equipment inspection, mechanical fault identification, engine disassembly, system repairs, workshop safety and organization, and supervised electrical diagnostics.

Development Toward Data Analytics

John is developing analytical capability in Excel, Power Query, SQL, SQLite, and Power BI, with Python as a developing capability. His professional direction is agricultural and environmental systems analysis through data — applying his engineering foundation to investigate food security, agricultural production, and environmental systems using evidence-driven methods.
