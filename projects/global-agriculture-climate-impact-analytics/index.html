Context & objective

The project explores a dataset spanning countries, crops, years, and adaptation strategies. Its objective is descriptive: organise the records into an interactive dashboard and examine patterns in yield, climate, and economic impact, without treating associations as real-world climate effects.

Data context

The 10,000-record dataset covers 10 countries, 10 crops, and five adaptation strategies across 1990–2024. The Kaggle listing does not provide a verified author, source, or license. The project notes that the pattern is consistent with simulated data, but that has not been established. Raw data is not included in the project repository or this website.

Analytical approach

In Excel, the source table is extended with three calculated fields for yield risk, temperature severity, and low-yield status. Five PivotTables feed four PivotCharts, four KPI cards, and slicers for year, country, and adaptation strategy. No macros, Power Query, data model, or external connections are used.

Limitations & availability

Source author, provenance, and reuse license are unverified; raw data is not redistributed. Yield and temperature thresholds are analyst-defined. The analysis is descriptive, correlations and ANOVA do not establish causation, and no claim is made about real-world climate impacts.
