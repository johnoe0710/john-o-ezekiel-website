Overview

A collaborative three-person final-year engineering project addressing mechanized cashew nut cracking. The project was jointly resourced by the team for fabrication, testing, and implementation.

Individual Contribution

John's individual contribution to the project was the structural design of the cracker. Performance evaluation and business/economic analysis were led by the other two team members.

Engineering Relevance

The project applies agricultural mechanization principles to a post-harvest processing problem, connecting structural engineering design to practical agricultural equipment.
